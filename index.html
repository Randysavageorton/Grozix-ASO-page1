Technical check

Why Your Website May Not Be Getting Found in AI Answers

Good content is only part of the story. Often the website itself keeps AI tools out, or confuses them. These are the six most common technical causes.

Technical problemWhat happensWhat it costs you
AI crawlers are blocked
Crawlers are the bots that read your pages. A robots.txt rule or a firewall setting can turn away bots like GPTBot or PerplexityBot.
AI tools cannot read your pages, so they cannot cite them.
Key content loads through JavaScript
Many AI crawlers do not run scripts. If your services or prices only appear after a script runs, the bot may see a near-empty page.
Your best content stays invisible to them.
Business facts are missing from your code
Schema is code that labels your name, services, location and reviews. Without it, tools have to guess.
They may describe you wrongly, or skip you.
Facts do not match across the web
Your site, Google profile and directories show different names, addresses or services.
AI tools lose trust and repeat old or wrong details.
Answers are buried
The page opens with a long intro. The real answer sits far down, under unclear headings.
Tools choose a rival page that answers faster.
Crawl and speed errors
Slow pages, broken links, long redirect chains, pages missing from your sitemap, or a "noindex" tag, which tells Google not to list a page.
Google and AI tools skip or drop your pages.

Each of these can be checked and fixed. Our free AI visibility audit looks for all six. Our AI-Ready Technical SEO and Entity & Schema Optimization work then fixes them.

What are AI search optimization services?+

AI search optimization services help your business get named, cited and described correctly in answers from ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews. The work covers your content, your business facts, your mentions on trusted sites and the tracking that shows what changed.

How are AI SEO services different from traditional SEO?+

Traditional SEO ranks pages in a list of links. AI SEO services aim to get your business named inside a written answer. The two share a foundation, so good SEO still helps. AI work adds clearer answers, consistent facts and more mentions across the web.
